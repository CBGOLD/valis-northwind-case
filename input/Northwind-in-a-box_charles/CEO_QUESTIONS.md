# Questions for the Operator

A few things I want a clear read on. Dig into the data we've got — the P&L export, the headcount roster, the Slack threads, the meeting notes, the support tickets — and come back with straight answers.

— Dana Whitfield, CEO

---

## Q1. SaaS spend

**"What did we actually spend on SaaS tools last quarter?"**

I want the real number for software/SaaS for Q1 2026. Finance has the P&L line items; pull it from there and tell me what we're paying for.

## Q2. Sales hiring

**"Did we decide to hire in Sales or freeze hiring — what's the current state, and who owns it?"**

There's been back-and-forth on this. I need to know where we landed, what the situation is right now, and who's accountable for the call.

## Workflow

**"Where are we wasting the most time, and what would you automate first?"**

Across the company — ops, finance, content, support — where is manual effort piling up? If you could automate one workflow, what would it be and why?
